# 🎹 Flinke Finger

A fun, gamified German typing trainer designed for kids (and adults who want to learn!).

[**🚀 Play Now / Jetzt Spielen**](https://dbartz.github.io/flinke-finger/)

## ✨ Features

- **RPG-Style Progression**: Level up from 1 to 100 based on your performance.
- **Unlockable Titles**: Earn 21 unique German titles like "Tasten-Neuling" to "Tipp-Legende".
- **60+ Lessons**: structured curriculum starting from home row to complex sentences and stories.
- **Gamified Feedback**:
  - ⭐ 1-3 Star rating system
  - 🔥 Streak mode with visual effects
  - 🎉 Confetti celebrations
  - 🔊 Satisfying mechanical keyboard sounds
- **Kid-Friendly UI**: Clean, dark mode interface with large text and no distractions.
- **Privacy Focused**: No tracking. All progress is saved locally on your device (`localStorage`).

## 🎮 How to Play

1.  **Select a Lesson**: Start with Level 1 if you are new to touch typing.
2.  **Type the Text**: Place your fingers on the home row (F and J have bumps!).
3.  **Earn Stars**:
    - ⭐: Complete the lesson
    - ⭐⭐: >90% Accuracy & >20 WPM
    - ⭐⭐⭐: >98% Accuracy & >30 WPM
4.  **Level Up**: Gather stars to increase your player level and unlock cool titles.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5 / CSS3 / Vanilla JavaScript**
- **No Frameworks**: Lightweight and fast.
- **Canvas Confetti**: For the celebrations.
- **Web Audio API**: For procedural sound generation.
